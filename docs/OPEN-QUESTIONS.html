Open questions

What is not known, or has been read in the code and not checked in the emulator.

What nothing points to

No instruction or word in the ROM points to these pieces. That does not prove they are unused: a computed address leaves no trace, and that already happened with the graphics of world 3's boss, which are picked with 0x6175 + 7 × step. None of this has been measured in openMSX:

  • Code: p00:405F and p00:4077; p02:8153; p03:A871, A904, AAC1, ADF7, AE23, B16F and B17E; p06:A1F8, ACE2, ADE5 and B183. The first two are entries of a jump vector nobody calls; the rest sit after a jp, a jr or a ret.
  • p04:6DC6: 56 bytes (16 pairs of steps and three orderings of 0-7), the same as p05:88FD.
  • p12:B850: 240 bytes identical, byte for byte, to the rle at 0xB760 (the seal in the boss room), right after it.

Room 94 of the castle

It has no neighbours: no room leads to it and it leads to none. On the map it goes in the only gap left between 93 and 95, but that is a deduction from the number, not a neighbour. p04:62A4 uses it as a generator. How to get in, if at all, has not been seen.

The demos

The table at p01:7DD9 has five demos. How many actually play, not measured.

Read in the code, not tried

  • Typing the boss's name. Seen in openMSX by setting the guessed flag (0xE020) by hand; by typing it, no.
  • UMBRELLA, the castle digits with item 0x27, item 0x15 with RETURN and world 8 without item 0x13.
  • The castle's hidden items (p02:9F82) and what each one asks for.
  • The password (p02:95A0): 43 letters of 5 bits plus 2 of checksum, in 11 groups of 4 and one of 1, typed in state 0x12 (p02:9716). How that screen is reached, not checked.
  • With Knightmare in the other slot it puts 99 in 0xE05A and 0xE05B; p02:908D copies them to 0xE053 and 0xE057 (the life caps). Not tried with the cartridge.
  • With the Game Master it sets bit 2 of 0xF0F8; what p00:5EE6 and p00:5F00 do with it, not looked at.

Not looked at

  • Items 0x12 and 0x14: 0x12 opens something in room 11 of world 2 (p08:9F87) and 0x14 makes something be drawn in the castle depending on 0xE06C (p00:5D24).
  • Each boss's life, weapon and damage. The life table (p01:7F97) goes by boss type, not by world, and type 5 does not exist: each type has to be matched to its map before giving the numbers per world.
  • A real machine. Everything is tested in openMSX with C-BIOS.
