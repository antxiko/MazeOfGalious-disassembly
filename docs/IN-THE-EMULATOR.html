In the emulator

Everything drawn on this site is checked against openMSX on a Japanese MSX1 machine (C-BIOS_MSX1_JP). The probes are Tcl scripts that take the cartridge wherever needed, by poking RAM or pressing real keys, and dump the VRAM (the first 16 KB) and the RAM. One emulator at a time.

To take the character to a room, omsx_mundos.tcl puts the map in 0xE041, the room in the four loaded neighbours (0xE648-0xE64B) and the character on the right edge (0xE507 = 0xF9): the game itself leaves through the right and loads the room. 0xE690 and 0xE740 must be cleared; otherwise the special room flag carries over and the next rooms are not built.

The checks

Tips for watching it

  • The Japanese machine shows the messages in Japanese; with C-BIOS_MSX1_EU they come out in English (p02:94F0 reads byte 0x002B of the BIOS).
  • openMSX screenshots can come out stale or black with the emulator sped up: the check is always done on the VRAM.
  • A game starts with space or M on the title (p00:4BFA).
  • F1 opens the item screen; F2, the pause (the one with the cup).

The Maze of Galious (Knightmare II) was published by Konami for the MSX in 1987; its catalogue number is RC-749 and it is 128 KB. All rights in the game remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
